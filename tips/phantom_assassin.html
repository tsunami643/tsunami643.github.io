Phantom Assassin

Tips

  • Understanding the ins and outs of the Stifling Dagger are critical to not getting demolished in lane. Learn the projectile's travel times, be able to accurately estimate the damage of the dagger at each level on creeps, and practice timing right clicks+melee range Daggers to make it impossible to deny you.
  • If an enemy in your lane has a Magic Stick, cast your Stifling Daggers from fog whenever possible. Stick only gains charges from spells that are cast in vision.
  • Stifling Dagger applies damage as an instant attack. This is a unique attack type that ignores disarms, has true strike, and applies attack modifiers. If you have an attack modifier with a proc, it'll roll for a chance once the projectile lands.
    • Since Stifling Dagger is an instant attack, it'll break your Smoke once it damages a unit.
    • You can activate Shadow Blade/Silver Edge after the Dagger projectile is launched to get the backstab damage.
  • Always be on the lookout for units to Phantom Strike to when you're trying to escape.
  • When pushing an uncontested tower, you can Phantom Strike onto a nearby enemy creep and use the attack speed buff to siege the tower.
  • When Blur is active, you won't show up on enemy minimaps, you're fully invisible, and have true sight immunity which means even sentry wards won't see you. However, if the enemy has their camera on something you're attacking, they can see the Coup de Grace blood effect and crit number.
  • When Blur is active, lane creeps and neutral creeps (except Rosh) don't react to getting attacked by you.
  • Activating Blur disjoints any incoming projectiles.
  • Blur's active dispelling follows the same rules as Smoke, but with a smaller radius. Meaning, it will dispel when near enemy heroes or towers.
    • Creep-heroes don't dispel Smoke and also don't dispel Blur. You can kill units like Visage's Familiars or Warlock's Golem while actively Blurred.
    • Unlike Smoke, you're given a small buffer time to exit the dispel radius to preserve Blur if you accidentally walk into range of an enemy.
  • Agh's Shard Blur lowering cooldowns on kills even works on Aegis deaths/Wraith King Reincarnation.
  • Agh's Scepter Fan of Knives pierces debuff immunity/BKB.
  • Agh's Scepter Fan of Knives absolutely shreds illusions because the damage calculates off of a target's max HP.
  • Reflected Blade Mail damage doesn't go through debuff immunity. If you're killing yourself on enemy Blade Mails, consider buying a BKB.

Counters

  • A Magic Stick may be a smart investment if you're doing a good job zoning her out since she'll have to spam Stifling Dagger for last hits.
  • If you're a carry, get a MKB or Bloodthorn for the accuracy to hit her through Immaterial evasion.
  • Attacks proccing Chain Lightning from Maelstrom/Mjollnir cannot be evaded. Not a huge bump in accuracy, but it's something.
  • The "Serrated Shiv" neutral item artifact can provide some accuracy against her evasion.
  • If you're a support, get a Ghost Scepter or Aeon Disk for survivability. A Pavise could allow you or an ally to survive a few more right clicks with the barrier. If you're tanky, get a Blade Mail so she'll kill herself on you.
  • Phantom Assassin's Immaterial evasion and Coup de Grace's Deadly Focus crit can both be disabled by breaks.
    • Breaks can be provided by Silver Edge or spells like Doom's Agh's Scepter Doom, Hoodwink's Sharpshooter, Naga Siren's Agh's Scepter Ensnare, Nyx Assassin's Vendetta, Phantom Assassin's Agh's Scepter Fan of Knives, Shadow Demon's Agh's Scepter Demonic Purge, Viper's Viper Strike, and a few others.
  • PA cannot Phantom Strike when rooted.
  • You can dispel Phantom Strike attack speed off of PA. This can make heroes like Shadow Demon with Demonic Purge or items like Nullifier handy counters.
  • The minimap is very deceptive when playing against a PA. You can never be 100% sure of her whereabouts because Blur's active hides her from your minimap.
    • Blur's active even gives PA true sight immunity, so if she's walking under sentry wards, you still won't see her.
    • However, if you move your camera over where you think she is, you should be able to see her Coup de Gras blood effects and crit number if she's attacking something.
